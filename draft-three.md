I have a problem.

The world produces too much information.

Important signals are hidden inside massive data streams.

I want to understand:

- Markets
- Weather
- Solar activity
- Lunar cycles
- Health economics
- Real estate

I do not want raw data.

I want knowledge.

The system should continuously collect information,
organize it,
identify relationships,
detect patterns,
and explain what matters.

The result should be insight, not data.
