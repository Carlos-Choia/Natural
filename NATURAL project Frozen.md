NATURAL

PROJECT STATUS: FROZEN Oct-2026

Reason:
The agents stage of development.

NATURAL served as an exploration of atomic, human-readable intent specifications (.nform)
for AI-based solution generation.

The ideas remain available for future evolution.

NATURAL was an exploration of a question:

How should humans communicate intent to AI systems?

The project introduced the concepts of:

- NATURAL-FORMS (.nform)
- Atomic intent documentation
- Human-readable specifications
- AI-interpreted solution generation
- Solution Architecture for the LLM era

As the AI industry evolves rapidly and commercial platforms begin to adopt specification-driven and agent-driven workflows, the project is being frozen.

The concepts remain available for future research, inspiration and possible future evolution.

NATURAL was never about replacing AI.

It was about exploring how humans could organize intent so that AI systems could execute it reliably.

Project frozen.
Ideas preserved.
Future open.
